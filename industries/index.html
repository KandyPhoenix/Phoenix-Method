Why industry-specific SEO matters

A local SEO playbook that works for a plumber doesn't automatically work for a therapist, a John Deere dealer, or an oilfield service company. Search intent, terminology, schema requirements, and trust signals all shift by industry. Treating SEO the same way across every business leaves rankings — and qualified traffic — on the table.

Terminology matters

How your customers search is industry-specific. Keyword research has to reflect your category's actual language, not generic templates.

Schema is specialized

MedicalBusiness, LocalBusiness, AutomotiveBusiness, Service — the structured data Google expects varies by industry.

Trust signals differ

YMYL industries need E-E-A-T. B2B industries need case-study depth. Local trades need review volume. Each category has its own trust-building motion.

Intent varies

A "near me" search in healthcare behaves differently than "near me" in oilfield services. Intent modeling has to fit your category's buying journey.
