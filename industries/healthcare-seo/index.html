Why Healthcare Is Different

Healthcare SEO Plays on Hard Mode

Google classifies healthcare content as YMYL — Your Money or Your Life. That means pages about medical conditions, treatments, mental health, or anything that could materially affect a reader's wellbeing are held to a higher evidentiary standard than almost anything else on the web. Generic "write more blog posts with keywords" advice isn't just ineffective here — it actively undermines your rankings.

On top of that, healthcare practices face regulatory layers most SEO agencies never think about — patient testimonials, tracking pixels on appointment forms, and analytics configurations are all regulatory territory. We stay entirely focused on SEO and never make decisions outside that scope.

The short version: healthcare needs an SEO partner who understands YMYL and stays carefully out of compliance territory. We write, optimize, and rank. We don't touch patient data and don't make regulatory decisions.

The Playbook

How Healthcare SEO Actually Gets Won

📋

Build E-E-A-T signals systematically

Author bios for every provider with credentials, certifications, and medical affiliations visible. About pages that establish institutional trust. Medical review by qualified practitioners when appropriate, with the reviewer visible. This is the single biggest lever for healthcare rankings and most competitors do it badly or not at all.

🔖

Deploy medical schema correctly

MedicalBusiness, MedicalClinic, Physician, MedicalCondition, MedicalTherapy, and MedicalProcedure schema — mapped to the specific pages that describe them. Structured data tells Google exactly what kind of healthcare entity this is and gets you into rich results most competitors aren't eligible for.

🗺️

Win the local map pack

For 90% of healthcare practices, local SEO drives the majority of high-intent patient search. NAP consistency, GBP optimization with healthcare-specific categories, review velocity, and local schema. See our Local SEO service for the full framework.

Write patient-focused content, not SEO-first copy

Written with clinical accuracy, optimized without stuffing. The content calendar is built around patient intent, not keyword volume alone.

Frequently Asked

Healthcare SEO Questions

What makes healthcare SEO different from regular SEO?

Healthcare content falls under Google's YMYL (Your Money or Your Life) category, which means it's held to a much higher standard for accuracy, credibility, and author expertise. Generic SEO playbooks often fail for healthcare because they miss E-E-A-T signals and medical schema requirements. Healthcare also carries regulatory layers that generalist agencies sometimes wander into. We don't — we handle SEO and stop there.

Will SEO work create compliance issues for my practice?

SEO work itself — content, keywords, schema, technical optimization — is generally fine and does not typically involve protected health information. The risky areas are adjacent to SEO: testimonial handling, tracking pixels on appointment forms, review widgets, analytics configurations. Those are regulatory territory we stay out of entirely. If we notice something on your existing site that looks compliance-sensitive, we flag it to you. We don't make regulatory decisions.

What is E-E-A-T and why does it matter for medical practices?

E-E-A-T stands for Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness. For YMYL topics like healthcare, Google's quality raters explicitly evaluate these signals. Content must be written or medically reviewed by qualified practitioners. Author bios with credentials must be visible on every piece of substantive content. The practice's institutional authority (accreditations, associations, peer-reviewed citations) must be discoverable. Practices that skip this plateau fast; practices that invest in it compound.

Can you help with Google Ads / PPC restrictions for treatment centers?

Our focus is organic SEO. Paid media is available as a custom-scoped engagement if you want coordinated paid and organic. Healthcare ad eligibility, certification, and restricted-category regulatory questions stay outside our scope — we don't make regulatory decisions.

Which practice types do you have experience with?

Mental health and therapy practices, dental and orthodontic offices, primary care and specialty medical clinics, rehab and addiction treatment centers, chiropractic, and healthcare-adjacent services. We bring a background in healthcare operations to every engagement and stay strictly in our SEO lane.

Do I need local SEO for my practice?

Yes — for almost every healthcare practice, local SEO is the foundation. Patients search "therapist near me", "dentist in [city]", "urgent care open now". If you're not in the map pack, you're losing intent-heavy searchers to competitors before they ever see your site. Healthcare SEO and local SEO work together; treat them as layers of the same strategy.

How long until I see results?

Map pack movement is typically visible within 30–60 days. Organic rankings for competitive medical queries take longer — 4–6 months to start, often 9–12 months to reach full authority for YMYL terms. Patience matters in healthcare SEO because the ranking signals compound. Practices that stick with the strategy for a year routinely outcompete larger networks that never invest in E-E-A-T.
