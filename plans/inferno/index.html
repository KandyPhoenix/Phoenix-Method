In Practice

What Inferno Looks Like Month-to-Month

A typical Inferno month:

  • Every week: Technical crawl runs across the whole site, your keywords get checked, and any new issues are flagged. Urgent requests handled same-day whenever possible.
  • Week 1: Competitor deep-scan and AI Overview eligibility check on your existing pages. Priorities for the month confirmed with you.
  • Through the month: Your 12 hours go to fixes, updates, and rewrites of existing pages. Schema deployed and maintained.
  • Month end: Keyword rankings recorded, AI Overview citation check, portal updated. Any remaining hours used on schema depth or site optimization.
  • Every quarter: Your report lands — what we fixed, what moved, and what comes next.

Inferno carries twice the hours of Blaze because a 50-page site has twice the surface to keep healthy. The schema and AI Overview work is where it pulls ahead.

Frequently Asked

Inferno Plan Questions

What do the 12 hours cover?

Technical fixes from the weekly audit, updates and rewrites of existing page content, schema and meta tag work, and changes you ask for on the pages you already have. We plan the mix with you each month based on what's going to move the needle most.

What if I use all 12 hours?

Extra work is $75/hr for plan clients (25% off the standard $100/hr rate), and we confirm the cost with you before any of it is billed.

What does "priority support" actually mean?

Urgent site changes and quick-turn requests handled same-day whenever possible. Same-day isn't guaranteed for every request (some things take longer by necessity), but Inferno clients get to the front of the queue and have a faster direct line than other tiers.

Is Inferno overkill for most small businesses?

For most, yes. Most small business sites are 25 pages or fewer and are better served by Spark or Blaze. Inferno is specifically for larger sites, multi-location operators, or businesses that want the schema and AI Overview layer maintained every month. If you're not sure, we'll be honest — Blaze is often the right answer.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Month-to-month. Cancel anytime and it stops at the next billing cycle.

Do you offer anything above Inferno?

Not as a standard plan. Sites over 50 pages get a custom quote. Clients who outgrow Inferno typically need paid media, dedicated PR, or regular new content — all available as add-ons or custom-scoped engagements we can coordinate on. We'll have an honest conversation about what you need next if you get to that point.

Can I add paid advertising to my retainer?

The retainer is organic SEO only, but paid advertising is available as a separately scoped custom engagement alongside your retainer if you want coordinated paid and organic.
