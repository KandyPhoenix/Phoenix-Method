Frequently Asked

Inferno Plan Questions

What exactly counts as a "content piece"?

An SEO blog post (typically 1,200-2,000 words), a new landing page built from scratch, or a substantial rewrite of an existing page. Schema-only work counts as hours but not as a "content piece." We plan the mix with you each month based on what's going to move the needle most.

What does "priority support" actually mean?

Urgent site changes, time-sensitive content, and quick-turn requests handled same-day whenever possible. Same-day isn't guaranteed for every request (some things take longer by necessity), but Inferno clients get to the front of the queue and have a faster direct line than other tiers.

Is Inferno overkill for most small businesses?

For most, yes. Most small businesses are better served by Blaze. Inferno is specifically for competitive markets, multi-location operators, or businesses with aggressive growth targets where the extra content volume and schema depth produce meaningful competitive advantage. If you're not sure, we'll be honest — Blaze is often the right answer.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Month-to-month through Square. Cancel anytime and it stops at the next billing cycle.

Do you offer anything above Inferno?

Not as a standard plan. Clients who outgrow Inferno typically need paid media, dedicated PR, or larger content output — all available as custom-scoped engagements we can coordinate on. We'll have an honest conversation about what you need next if you get to that point.

Can I add paid advertising to my retainer?

The retainer is organic SEO only, but paid advertising is available as a separately scoped custom engagement alongside your retainer if you want coordinated paid and organic.
