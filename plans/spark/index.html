In Practice

What Spark Looks Like Month-to-Month

A typical Spark month in the life of a small business client:

  • Every week: Technical crawl runs, your keywords get checked, and any new issues are flagged.
  • Through the month: Your 2 hours go to the highest-impact fixes and updates — audit findings first, then the changes you have asked for.
  • Month end: Keyword rankings recorded for the month. Any remaining site work wrapped up.
  • Every quarter: Your report lands — what we fixed, what moved, and what comes next.

Most of the work happens quietly. You'll hear from us when there's something you need to know or approve — not just to fill inbox space with activity reports.

Do I need a setup first, or can I jump straight into Spark?

Technically you can start Spark without a setup, but if your site has technical issues or an unoptimized GBP, you'll spend months of retainer time fixing foundations that a one-time setup handles up front. A setup takes one to three months, depending on the size of your site. Most clients start with the $800 SEO Foundation Setup, then transition into Spark. See setups →

Can I upgrade later if Spark isn't enough?

Yes, any time. Upgrading from Spark to Blaze or Inferno is one click — we swap the Square subscription and scale up deliverables the next month.

What if I need more than 2 hours of work in a given month?

Buy additional à la carte — extra work is $75/hr for retainer clients (25% off the standard $100/hr rate). Or upgrade to Blaze for the month if you're consistently hitting the ceiling.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Month-to-month, no contracts. Cancel through Square or by email and it stops at the next billing cycle.

Does Spark include link building?

Not as a core deliverable. Link building requires its own scope and budget. Retainer content naturally attracts links over time, and we handle citation-style links as part of local SEO work. For proactive link building campaigns, that's typically Blaze or Inferno scope plus add-on budget.
