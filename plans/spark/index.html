Who It's For

Is Spark the Right Fit?

Spark is the right plan if you're a small business that:

  • Already has a functioning website that converts OK when people find it
  • Wants to hold current rankings and not lose ground to competitors
  • Needs someone watching your GBP, tracking rankings, and catching technical regressions
  • Has a small content output appetite — one blog post or set of site updates per month is enough
  • Wants the lowest-cost ongoing SEO maintenance that actually works
  • Is either just starting SEO or running a stable business without aggressive growth goals

If you're in a competitive market trying to climb fast, or a high-growth practice in a crowded niche, you'll outgrow Spark quickly. Most of those businesses start at Blaze ($900/mo) instead.

In Practice

What Spark Looks Like Month-to-Month

A typical Spark month in the life of a small business client:

  • Week 1: Monthly technical crawl runs, any new issues flagged and fixed if quick wins. Keyword tracking refreshed.
  • Weeks 2-3: GBP monitored, reviews responded to if not already handled, any profile issues corrected. Content work for the month executed — typically one new blog post or a significant page update.
  • Week 4: Any remaining site work wrapped up. Quarterly report compiled (every third month).

Most of the work happens quietly. You'll hear from us when there's something you need to know or approve — not just to fill inbox space with activity reports.

Realistic Expectations

What Spark Will and Won't Do

Will: Hold your current rankings against algorithm updates and competitor moves. Catch technical regressions early. Keep your GBP active and well-maintained. Produce steady, incremental content that compounds over time.

Won't: Move you from invisible to dominant overnight. Win aggressive competitive markets through sheer content volume. Replace a needed foundation investment (if your site has no technical foundation, start with one of our setup packages before a retainer).

When to upgrade to Blaze

Can I upgrade later if Spark isn't enough?

Yes, any time. Upgrading from Spark to Blaze or Inferno is one click — we swap the Square subscription and scale up deliverables the next month.

What if I need more than 2 hours of content in a given month?

Buy additional à la carte — content work is $100/hr for retainer clients (25% off standard rate). Or upgrade to Blaze for the month if you're consistently hitting the ceiling.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Month-to-month, no contracts. Cancel through Square or by email and it stops at the next billing cycle.

Does Spark include link building?

Not as a core deliverable. Link building requires its own scope and budget. Retainer content naturally attracts links over time, and we handle citation-style links as part of local SEO work. For proactive link building campaigns, that's typically Blaze or Inferno scope plus add-on budget.
