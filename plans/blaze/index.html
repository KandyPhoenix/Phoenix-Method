In Practice

What Blaze Looks Like Month-to-Month

A typical Blaze month:

  • Every week: Technical crawl runs across the whole site, your keywords get checked, and any new issues are flagged.
  • Week 1: Competitor check — anything they published or gained in the last 30 days. Fix and update priorities for the month confirmed with you.
  • Through the month: Your 6 hours go to the highest-impact fixes and updates. Client portal updated with progress.
  • Month end: Keyword rankings recorded for the month. Any leftover hours used on schema, meta tags, or other site optimization.
  • Every quarter: Your report lands — what we fixed, what moved, and what comes next.

The extra hours are where Blaze earns its keep. A 25-page site throws off more issues and needs more upkeep than a 10-page one. Six hours a month keeps all of it current.

Frequently Asked

Blaze Plan Questions

What do the 6 hours cover?

Technical fixes from the weekly audit, updates to existing page content, schema and meta tag work, and changes you ask for on the pages you already have. We plan the priorities with you at the start of each month.

What if I use all 6 hours?

Extra work is $75/hr for plan clients (25% off the standard $100/hr rate), and we confirm the cost with you before any of it is billed.

What if my site is bigger than 25 pages?

Inferno covers sites of up to 50 pages. Above that, we quote it first.

Is the competitor surveillance actually useful?

Depends on your market. In competitive niches (dental, mental health, legal, trades), knowing what your top 3 competitors are publishing and ranking for is tactical gold. In low-competition markets, it's more informational. Either way, it takes us roughly an hour a month and produces a report you can see in your portal.

What is the client portal exactly?

A personalized Notion page (or equivalent) showing current rankings for your tracked keywords, the current month's work with status, links to reports, billing status, and a direct messaging section. Updated monthly, accessible 24/7.

Can I choose where the hours go?

Yes. Some months it's all audit fixes. Other months you may want page copy refreshed, schema added, or a list of changes you've been saving up. We plan monthly with you.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Month-to-month. Cancel anytime and it stops at the next billing cycle.

Does Blaze include ad management or paid media?

No — the Blaze retainer is organic SEO only. Paid media is available as a separately scoped custom engagement if you want coordinated paid and organic work.
