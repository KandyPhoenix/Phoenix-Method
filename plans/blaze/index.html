In Practice

What Blaze Looks Like Month-to-Month

A typical Blaze month:

  • Week 1: Technical crawl runs. Competitor check — anything they published or gained in the last 30 days. Content plan for the month confirmed with you.
  • Weeks 2-3: Content execution — typically two blog posts, OR one blog + one landing page, OR a major rewrite of an existing page. GBP check runs weekly. Client portal updated with progress.
  • Week 4: Rank tracking review, site updates wrapped up, any leftover hours used on schema, meta tags, or other site optimization. Quarterly report compiled (every third month).

The content cadence is where Blaze earns its keep. Two substantive content pieces per month, sustained over time, is what builds the topic authority that produces compounding organic traffic.

Frequently Asked

Blaze Plan Questions

What do the 2 content pieces look like?

Most commonly: two SEO blog posts targeting specific keyword clusters you want to rank for. Alternatively: one blog post + one landing page, one major page rewrite + one blog, or two landing pages if you're building out services. We plan the content calendar with you at the start of each month.

Is the competitor surveillance actually useful?

Depends on your market. In competitive niches (dental, mental health, legal, trades), knowing what your top 3 competitors are publishing and ranking for is tactical gold. In low-competition markets, it's more informational. Either way, it takes us roughly an hour a month and produces a report you can see in your portal.

What is the client portal exactly?

A personalized Notion page (or equivalent) showing current rankings for your tracked keywords, deliverables for the current month with status, content calendar, links to reports, billing status, and a direct messaging section. Updated monthly, accessible 24/7.

Can I adjust which services the content hours go toward?

Yes — that's the point of the "flexible to what moves your needle" language. Some months you may want both hours in blog posts. Other months you may want one blog and one landing page. Or schema work instead of new content. We plan monthly with you.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Month-to-month through Square. Cancel anytime and it stops at the next billing cycle.

Does Blaze include ad management or paid media?

No — the Blaze retainer is organic SEO only. Paid media is available as a separately scoped custom engagement if you want coordinated paid and organic work.
