**FREE AUDIT = The Diagnosis** (15-20 min, sells the strategy call)

**FULL REPORT = The Prescription** (2-4 hours, paying clients ONLY)
- Complete roadmap with exact fixes
- Includes: everything in free audit PLUS how to fix each issue, phased action plan, keyword data, content strategy, implementation timeline

## FREE AUDIT TEMPLATE

**To run:** Tell Claude "Run a free SEO audit on [website URL]"

### Process (15-20 minutes)
1. **Ahrefs** (3 min) — Site Explorer > enter domain > note DR, organic traffic, keywords, referring domains, top 2 competitors
2. **PageSpeed** (2 min) — pagespeed.web.dev > paste homepage URL > screenshot score
3. **Rich Results** (1 min) — search.google.com/test/rich-results > check for schema
4. **Google** (2 min) — Search their main keyword > note where they rank > screenshot competitors above them
5. **Claude** (5 min) — Ask Claude to crawl the site and check title tags, meta descriptions, H1s, alt text, internal links
6. **Fill template** (5 min) — Grade each category A-F, fill in findings

### Grading Cheat Sheet
| Grade | Meaning | When to Use |
|-------|---------|-------------|
| A | Excellent, no major issues | Rare for prospects |
| B | Good foundation, some gaps | Partial optimization done |
| C | Average, significant work needed | Losing to competitors |
| D | Poor, major issues, losing traffic | Most small businesses |
| F | Critical failure, invisible to Google | Best selling point |

### Quick Category Grading
- **Technical SEO:** Has schema? Speed ok? Mobile ok? Sitemap? → Most sites = D/F
- **On-Page:** Title tags? Meta desc? H1 per page? Alt text? → Typical = C/D
- **Local SEO:** GBP claimed? NAP consistent? Citations? Reviews? → Most = C/D
- **Content:** Pages long enough? FAQ sections? Blog? → Thin content = D/F
- **Speed:** PageSpeed score 0-49=F, 50-69=D, 70-89=C/B, 90+=A
- **Backlinks:** DR under 10=F, 10-20=D, 20-40=C, 40-60=B, 60+=A
- **E-E-A-T:** Author bios? Credentials? Reviews? Trust signals? → Most = C/D

## FULL AUDIT TEMPLATE (PAYING CLIENTS ONLY)

**To run:** Tell Claude "Build a full SEO audit report for [website URL] — this is a paying client"

### Process (2-4 hours)
1. **Ahrefs** (15 min) — Full Site Explorer: DR, all organic keywords (export top 50), traffic history, backlink profile, referring domains, competitor comparison (top 3)
2. **Claude site crawl** (20 min) — Give Claude the URL. Get: page inventory, title tags, meta descriptions, heading structure, content quality, internal linking analysis
3. **PageSpeed** (10 min) — Test homepage + 3-5 key pages. Document all CWV issues with numbers
4. **Rich Results** (10 min) — Test every major page type. Document missing schema opportunities
5. **GSC/GA4** (15 min) — If access granted: impressions, clicks, CTR, avg position, top queries, crawl errors
6. **Manual Review** (30 min) — E-E-A-T signals, trust indicators, content depth, FAQ opportunities, local SEO, mobile UX
7. **Claude report generation** (20 min) — Feed all data to Claude, generate the full report
8. **Review & customize** (20 min) — Add personal notes, client-specific recommendations
