What Happens After You Pay

The same four moves we run on every site, sized for the Spark plan.

  1. Week 1

    Autopsy & Recon Us

    We dissect the whole site, expose every broken piece and pin down exactly where you rank today. Nothing hides. Nothing survives unexamined.

    • Broken links
    • Slow pages
    • Missing tags
    • Indexing problems
    • Starting rankings

    Your job: nothing.

  2. Week 1

    Battle Plan Us

    Everything we found gets sorted into a strike list. The fixes that move rankings fastest go first.

    Your job: nothing.

  3. Weeks 2–4

    Resurrect Us

    Fixes go in. Your pages get updated. The site gets a technical audit every week, and your keywords get checked with it.

    • 2 hours of site fixes and page updates
    • Weekly technical audit
    • Keywords checked every audit

    Your job: nothing. Have a request? Send an email.

  4. Every month

    Rise Us

    The cycle repeats. Rankings are tracked every month and checked during every weekly audit, so every move is on record. SEO compounds, and how fast depends on your market and where you started.

    Your job: run your business.

  5. Every quarter

    Your Report Us

    Every three months you get a report: what we fixed, what moved, what is next.

    Your job: read it.
