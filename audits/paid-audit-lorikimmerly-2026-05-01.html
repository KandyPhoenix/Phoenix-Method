Executive Summary

Lori Kimmerly Therapy is a licensed therapy practice based in Renton, Washington, offering individual, couples, and family therapy both in-person and via telehealth across Washington state and Arizona. The practice employs a diverse team of more than a dozen licensed and associate-level therapists, with specializations spanning Internal Family Systems (IFS), anxiety, depression, trauma, couples counseling, and culturally responsive care.

This audit covers every publicly indexed page on lorikimmerly.com — 22 pages crawled across 6 depth levels — analyzing technical infrastructure, on-page optimization, schema markup, content strategy, E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness) signals, and local SEO posture. The site's overall foundation is solid: HTTPS is enforced, redirects are correctly configured, and a sitemap is present. However, the audit reveals critical gaps in content architecture, schema implementation, and trust signals that are directly preventing the site from ranking competitively for its most valuable keywords.

As a mental health practice, lorikimmerly.com is classified by Google as a YMYL (Your Money or Your Life) site — a category subject to the highest levels of quality scrutiny under Google's Search Quality Rater Guidelines. For YMYL sites, E-E-A-T signals are not optional enhancements; they are ranking prerequisites. The practice currently lacks a Privacy Policy page, on-site patient testimonials, and structured content that demonstrates clinical depth — all areas that direct competitors have prioritized and that Google's quality evaluators actively look for.

Page Speed

⚠ Manual Speed Test Required

PageSpeed Insights API quota was exhausted during this audit. Page speed must be checked manually. WordPress + Avada theme sites typically score 45–65 on mobile without optimization. Common issues include render-blocking JS, unoptimized images, and Avada's heavy CSS/JS payload.

Fix: Run manual test at pagespeed.web.dev for lorikimmerly.com. Target: 70+ mobile, 85+ desktop. Key metrics: LCP under 2.5s, CLS under 0.1, TBT under 200ms. Likely quick wins: enable lazy loading, compress images with Smush or ShortPixel, enable Avada's performance mode and async JS loading.

Schema Markup — Technical Implementation

✓ MedicalBusiness Schema on Homepage

Homepage includes a MedicalBusiness schema object — the correct type for a therapy practice. This is a meaningful signal to Google about the nature of the business.

✓ Person Schema on Therapist Bios

Person schema is present on most therapist bio pages, correctly identifying each therapist. This supports rich result eligibility for individual practitioner searches.

✗ No LocalBusiness or MedicalClinic Schema on Key Pages

While MedicalBusiness is on the homepage, no LocalBusiness or MedicalClinic schema appears on the About page or Contact page — the two pages most likely to appear in local results. LocalBusiness schema with NAP (Name, Address, Phone) and opening hours is a critical local SEO signal.

Fix: Add LocalBusiness schema (type: "MedicalClinic" or "MentalHealthCare") to the About and Contact pages with: name, address, telephone, url, areaServed, openingHoursSpecification. Use Rank Math's Schema tab or add via JSON-LD block.

✗ No WebSite Schema / Sitelinks Searchbox

No WebSite schema is present. This prevents Google from potentially displaying a sitelinks search box in branded search results.

Fix: Add WebSite schema to homepage with url and potentialAction SearchAction targeting the site's search functionality.

⚠ 5 Bio Pages Missing Person Schema

Jared Alleger, Clayton Holman, Natalie Kim, Mariah Visco, and Caroline Bacon (bio page schema shows Unknown or duplicate Person objects) need their Person schema verified and corrected in Rank Math.

Fix: In Rank Math → each bio page → Schema → verify Person schema is present with: name, jobTitle, image, url. For interns, do not include "alumniOf" claims beyond actual credentials.

E-E-A-T Assessment (Critical for YMYL)

C Grade: C — Credentials Present, Trust Infrastructure Incomplete

Mental health practices are among the highest-stakes YMYL categories in Google's Search Quality Rater Guidelines. People searching for a therapist are making a decision that can profoundly affect their wellbeing — and Google's quality evaluators are trained to scrutinize such sites rigorously. The practice has real strengths here (licensed therapists, credentials displayed, insurance accepted) but is missing several infrastructure-level trust signals that top-ranking competitors have prioritized.

What's Working

✓ Therapist Credentials Clearly Displayed

All 12 therapist bio pages include licensure credentials (LMFT, LMFTA, LMHCA, SUDP-T). This is the most important E-E-A-T signal for a therapy practice and is being executed well. The bio cards include credential suffixes and areas of specialization.

✓ Insurance Accepted — Listed on Homepage

Insurance information (Premera, Cigna, Aetna, Kaiser) is visible on the homepage, which reduces friction for visitors and signals practice legitimacy.

✓ Phone Number Visible on Homepage

(253) 347-2579 is present on the homepage — a key local trust signal and NAP consistency anchor.

✓ Map Embed on Contact Page

Google Maps embed on the Contact page confirms a real physical location — important for local E-E-A-T signals.

Critical Gaps

✗ No Privacy Policy Page (CRITICAL for YMYL)

A Privacy Policy page is not present in the crawl. For a healthcare/YMYL site that collects contact form data and likely uses analytics, the absence of a Privacy Policy is both a legal compliance risk and an E-E-A-T red flag. Google's quality evaluators specifically check for privacy policies on YMYL sites. HIPAA requirements also demand a clear Notice of Privacy Practices for covered entities.

Fix (Week 1): Create /privacy-policy/ with a full HIPAA-compliant Notice of Privacy Practices. Use a licensed healthcare attorney template or a HIPAA privacy notice generator. Add to footer navigation. Length: 600+ words minimum.

✗ No On-Site Patient Testimonials or Reviews

No testimonials or reviews section exists anywhere on the site. Competitors with 5-star Google ratings prominently feature social proof. While HIPAA restricts using patient quotes in identifiable contexts, anonymous testimonials and star ratings embedded via third-party widgets (Psychology Today, Google) are widely used by top-ranking practices.

Fix (Phase 2): Add a testimonials section to the homepage and Contact page using anonymous quotes (e.g., "Client, 2025 — Anxiety"). Embed a Google Reviews widget if ratings are present. Reference Psychology Today profile if listed there.

⚠ Physical Address Not on Homepage

The practice's street address is not visible on the homepage. The phone number is present, but the address only appears on the Contact page. For local SEO, NAP (Name, Address, Phone) should be consistently visible in the footer of every page.

Fix: Add the full NAP to the site footer: "Lori Kimmerly Therapy | [Street Address] Renton, WA [ZIP] | (253) 347-2579". Wrap in LocalBusiness schema. This will appear on every page automatically.

⚠ Blog Post Authors Show as SEO Team, Not Therapists

The blog post about IFS therapy is authored by "PMSEO" (the Phoenix Method SEO account), not a therapist. For YMYL content on mental health topics, Google's quality raters expect the author to have verifiable clinical credentials. Having an SEO account as author creates an E-E-A-T liability for that content.

Fix: Reassign blog posts to the "lktherapy" author account, and build out that author profile with: full name ("Lori Kimmerly Therapy Staff"), credentials, bio, and a link to the About page. Alternatively, create individual author accounts for each contributing therapist and use their bio pages as the author URL.

⚠ No Terms of Service / Disclaimer

No Terms of Service or therapy disclaimer page is present. A standard therapy site disclaimer clarifies that website content is not therapy and not a substitute for professional treatment — important for YMYL and liability reasons.

Fix: Create a /terms-of-service or /disclaimer page (400+ words). Add to footer navigation alongside Privacy Policy.
