If there's one thing we want you to take away from this report, it's this: building dedicated service pages is the single action that will move your rankings more than anything else combined.

Right now, when someone in Renton searches "IFS therapist near me" or "anxiety therapy Washington State," your site doesn't have a page that directly answers that search. Google needs a page, and your competitors who do have those pages are taking that traffic. Here's how this plays out:

Pages to Build — What Each One Unlocks

Anxiety Therapy Renton WA — One of the highest-searched therapy terms in your area. A dedicated page targeting this term with your therapists' credentials and specializations addresses a direct gap in your current content.

Couples Counseling Renton WA — High commercial intent: people searching this are ready to book. Linking this page to your couples-specialized therapist bios makes it even stronger.

The #2 Opportunity: Local Visibility

Google Business Profile — Your Free Storefront

When someone searches "therapist near me" on Google, the map results that appear above everything else are driven by Google Business Profile. A complete, active GBP listing with your services listed, photos uploaded, and regular posts is one of the fastest ways to appear in front of local searchers. Your competitors who appear in the local map pack are there largely because of a well-maintained GBP — not necessarily because their website is better than yours.

What a complete GBP does for you: Appears in local map searches · Shows your phone number, address, and hours without the client needing to visit your site · Displays Google reviews · Posts show mental health tips and office updates · Pre-answered Q&A builds trust before first contact

Therapy Directories — Where Your Clients Are Already Looking

Psychology Today, TherapyDen, and Healthgrades are the directories that therapy clients specifically use when searching for a provider. Each listing is also a citation that reinforces your practice's legitimacy with Google. Every therapist on your team should have a profile on Psychology Today — it's the single most-used directory in your field and appears at or near the top of nearly every local therapy search.
