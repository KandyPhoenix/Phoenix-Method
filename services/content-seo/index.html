What It Actually Is

Content SEO Is the Difference Between Visibility and Volume

Anyone can publish content. Very few businesses publish content that earns rankings and generates revenue. The gap between "we post weekly" and "our blog generates 40% of our leads" isn't about volume — it's about strategy, structure, and execution.

Content SEO starts with keyword research that identifies what your customers actually search for (not what you assume they search for). It maps those keywords into topic clusters — pillar pages supported by related posts — that signal topical authority to Google. Each piece is written with clear intent: answer the question, cover the subject completely, link to relevant next steps. Each piece is technically optimized: headers that establish hierarchy, schema that earns rich results, internal links that distribute authority. Each piece is edited for accuracy by someone who actually knows the industry.

How We Write

The Editorial Standard

Every piece we produce meets these non-negotiables:

  • Subject matter accuracy — fact-checked against authoritative sources; no hallucinated statistics or fake citations
  • Search intent match — written to serve the actual query (informational, commercial, navigational)
  • Scannable structure — clear H-hierarchy, short paragraphs, bulleted lists, highlighted takeaways
  • Internal linking — every new piece connects to at least 2-3 existing related pages, funneling authority
  • Schema markup — Article, FAQ, HowTo, or whichever fits — deployed at publish time, not as an afterthought
  • Meta optimization — title, description, canonical, Open Graph, Twitter card all set correctly
  • Plain English, not SEO-speak — written for humans; keyword stuffing is a 2015 tactic Google now penalizes

Frequently Asked

Content SEO Questions

How many blog posts should I publish per month?

Quality over frequency. 2–4 substantive, well-researched posts per month outperform 20 thin posts. We'd rather build one piece of content that ranks and drives leads for years than churn out filler.

What's a topic cluster and why does it matter?

A topic cluster is a pillar page plus a constellation of supporting pages on related subtopics, all internally linked. Google interprets this as topical authority — the site that covers a subject comprehensively ranks better than the site with one thin post on it. Topic clusters are how you earn authority in competitive niches.

Can I just use AI to write SEO content?

AI-generated content that gets edited by a human who knows the subject can work. Raw AI output published without editorial rigor gets detected (by Google and by readers) and hurts more than it helps. We use AI as a tool, but every piece that goes out is human-edited with subject-matter accuracy checked.

How long does content SEO take to work?

Individual posts typically start ranking for long-tail queries within 2–3 months. Topic cluster authority for competitive terms compounds over 6–12 months. Content is a long game — but it's the game that produces durable organic traffic years after publication.

Do you do content for YMYL topics (health, finance, legal)?

Yes — we have particular depth in healthcare content. YMYL topics require extra E-E-A-T work: author credentials, demonstrable expertise, and authoritative citations. See our Healthcare SEO page for how we handle this.

Can you refresh old posts instead of writing new ones?

Often that's the better investment. Refreshing posts that are already ranking on page 2 or 3 — adding depth, updating data, improving internal links, refining on-page — frequently produces faster traffic gains than net-new content. We audit what you have first and recommend the mix.
