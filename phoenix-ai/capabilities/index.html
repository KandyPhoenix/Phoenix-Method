End-to-End

From Sign-In to Live Article

One pipeline, six steps. Most of it runs without you after the initial connect.

Sign in

Magic-link email or Google. No password to remember.

Connect

Drop your site URL, pick a CMS. Phoenix scrapes the homepage and learns your niche + brand voice.

Pick a keyword source

Google Search Console, Ahrefs (yours or ours), SerpBear, or manual keyword chips. Mix and match.

Research

We pull candidates, then an LLM relevance judge drops anything off-niche before it hits your chip list.

Generate

One click. ~20 seconds. Title, ~1500-word body, meta description, FAQs, and a 1280×720 hero image (FLUX).

Publish

Pushes to your site in the format your CMS expects — see the matrix below. Sitemap updated, IndexNow pinged, ready to rank.

Every Article Ships With

The Technical SEO Checklist

This is the per-article list. Anything you'd want a senior SEO to manually verify on every post — already there, every time.

Page-level meta

  • Title tag with brand suffix (smart de-dup)
  • Meta description tuned to the keyword
  • Canonical URL
  • Robots: index, follow, max-image-preview:large
  • Charset + viewport (mobile-ready)
  • <html lang="en">

Open Graph + Twitter (social previews)

  • og:title / og:description / og:url / og:type=article
  • og:site_name (your brand)
  • og:image with explicit 1280×720 dimensions
  • article:published_time / article:modified_time / article:author
  • twitter:card=summary_large_image + twitter:image

Structured data (JSON-LD)

  • Article schema (headline, description, image, dates, author, publisher)
  • BreadcrumbList (Home → Blog → Article)
  • FAQPage (when the article has FAQs — most do)
  • inLanguage, preserved datePublished, fresh dateModified

Content + media

  • ~1500-word body in your brand voice
  • Proper H1 / H2 / H3 hierarchy
  • FAQ section pulled from real search queries
  • 1280×720 hero image with alt text and dimensions (no CLS)
  • Eager-loaded hero (LCP-friendly)
Site-Wide

Beyond the Page — Discovery + Indexing

Great meta is wasted if Google can't find the post. Phoenix does the off-page work too.

Discovery

  • Sitemap.xml auto-rewrite on every publish (where we own the sitemap)
  • IndexNow ping to Bing, Yandex, Seznam, Naver — usually indexed within minutes
  • Marker-gated: we never overwrite your existing or generator-built sitemap

Operations

  • Daily cron — one fresh article per active site per day, as long as you have keyword inventory
  • Approve-before-publish mode (or full auto-publish, your call)
  • Per-site keyword blocklist (drop a chip → never suggested again)
  • Manual keyword chips on top of any primary source — your knowledge of the niche overlays our research
  • Full audit log of every pipeline run for debugging

Preserved publish dates: regenerating an article keeps Google's freshness/age trust signal. Most "AI content" tools rewrite the publish date every time, which silently resets your domain's article age signal.

Roadmap

What's Next

Honest about what we haven't built yet. Order roughly reflects what we'll ship next based on what beta users hit.

Next

Delete-from-SSG

Deleting a Jekyll/Hugo post from the dashboard currently misses the file in your repo. Manual cleanup needed until we ship this — usually one click.

Next

Brand chrome auto-bootstrap

For plain-HTML GitHub Pages sites without a theme convention, generate a starter site-chrome.css from your existing pages so new posts inherit your design from day one.

Soon

WordPress head meta injection

We currently push the body content; SEO plugins handle the head. Optionally inject our Article schema directly into the body for sites without Yoast/RankMath.

Soon

Eleventy / Astro / Gatsby

Same SSG detection pattern as Jekyll/Hugo. Will land as customers onboard with these stacks.

Soon

Customer-visible SSG override

If our detection guesses wrong, the override is set programmatically today. We'll surface it on the site detail form.

Soon

Multi-site billing

Beta limit is one site per account. Billing wiring + multi-site UX are needed before paid tiers open.
