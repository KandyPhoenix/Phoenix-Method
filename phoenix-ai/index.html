Frequently Asked

Common Questions

How is this different from Soro AI or other autopilot tools?

Same general category, different DNA. Soro is a software company. Phoenix AI is built by a working SEO agency — every editorial guardrail, internal-linking rule, and schema choice is what we use for paying SEO clients. We also dogfood it: phoenixmethodseo.com runs on Phoenix AI, so we feel every flaw before you do.

Will Google penalize AI-written content?

Google's policy is about quality, not method. Articles that match search intent, cite real sources, follow proper structure, and don't keyword-stuff rank fine — regardless of who (or what) wrote them. Phoenix AI is engineered to those guardrails. We use Claude (Anthropic) because its output is harder to detect as AI and easier to keep factually disciplined.

Do I have to use WordPress?

Today, yes — Phase 1 only supports WordPress (self-hosted or WordPress.com). Shopify, Webflow, and Ghost adapters are on the roadmap.

What about images?

The Ember and Blaze plans publish with a single header image picked from a stock library. Inferno adds AI-generated hero images that match your brand style.

Can I review articles before they publish?

Yes — by default, articles publish as drafts in WordPress. You review and click publish. Once you trust the output, you can flip auto-publish on for hands-off operation.

What if I want a human in the loop?

You're already in our wheelhouse. Phoenix Method offers human-written, human-edited content through our Content SEO service — start there if you need oversight on every piece. Phoenix AI is for sites that need volume on autopilot.
