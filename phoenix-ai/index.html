How It'll Work

Four Steps. Then It Runs Itself.

Connect

Sign in with email and connect your site. WordPress at launch, with Squarespace, Ghost, and GitHub-static support on the roadmap.

Learn

Phoenix AI reads your site to learn your niche, target audience, and brand voice — the way you write, the words you use, the angle you take.

Research

We'll pull keywords from Ahrefs scored for buyer intent and ranking opportunity in your niche, then cluster them into a topic-driven content calendar.

Publish

Phoenix AI will write a new SEO article on schedule — title, meta, H-hierarchy, Article schema, internal links — and push it to your site as a draft (or live, if you prefer).

What It'll Do

The Full SEO Content Stack — Automated

Keyword Research

Ahrefs-powered keyword discovery for your niche. Volume, KD, intent, and competitor gap analysis — refreshed monthly.

Brand Voice Learning

We analyze your existing site to model your tone, vocabulary, sentence rhythm, and POV. Output sounds like you, not a robot.

Long-form SEO Articles

1,500–2,500 word pieces with proper H-hierarchy, meta title, meta description, Article schema, and search-intent matching.

Internal Linking

Each new article links to 2–4 relevant existing pages on your site. Authority compounds across your domain.

Topic Clusters

Articles aren't picked at random. They're sequenced into pillar-and-cluster patterns Google rewards as topical authority.

WordPress Publishing

Pushes directly to your WordPress blog via the REST API — categories, tags, featured image, meta, schema all set on publish.

Review Before Publish

Set to draft-only and approve each piece, or flip auto-publish on once you trust the output. You're in control.

Daily Logs

Every pipeline run is logged with keyword picked, prompt used, time to generate, and publish status. Debug-friendly.

Frequently Asked

Common Questions

When can I actually use it?

We don't have a date yet. We're using ourselves and a handful of Phoenix Method clients as the first cohort to harden the pipeline before opening it up. If you're on the waitlist, you'll hear from us before we go public.

How is this different from other AI content tools?

Same general category, different DNA. Most AI content tools are built by software companies that don't run SEO for a living. Phoenix AI is built by a working SEO agency — every editorial guardrail, internal-linking rule, and schema choice is what we use for paying clients. The whole point of the private beta is to prove that out on real sites before opening it up.

Will Google penalize AI-written content?

Google's policy is about quality, not method. Articles that match search intent, cite real sources, follow proper structure, and don't keyword-stuff rank fine — regardless of who (or what) wrote them. Phoenix AI is engineered to those guardrails.

What sites will it work with?

WordPress at launch (self-hosted or WordPress.com). Squarespace, Ghost, and static GitHub-hosted sites are on the roadmap.

What if I need content now, not later?

You're in our wheelhouse. Phoenix Method already offers human-written, human-edited content through our Content SEO service. That's available today.
