## Post (paste-ready)

Therapist website management includes the links nobody sees.

An autopsy finding that's easy to miss:

A solid blog post on panic attacks. Written late at night by the clinician. Linking to nothing.

Not the anxiety therapy page. Not the booking page. Nothing.

That post is a dead end. Google reads internal links to work out which page on your site owns a topic. When the blog never points at the service page, the service page never gets the credit.

We wire our own site this way. Every post we publish links to the service pages for its topic. Both of this month's posts point at our mental health SEO page, which pulled 4,557 impressions in Google Search Console in the 90 days ending Oct 4, 2026.

Content SEO, the way we run it, is two jobs. Write the post. Then wire it into every page you want found. The second job is the one that gets skipped, and it's the one that makes the first one pay off.
