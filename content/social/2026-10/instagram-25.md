## Caption (paste-ready, Instagram)

Your website is somebody's job. Whose?

Every practice site is run by one of four people. Each one breaks in a predictable place.

The office manager: website tasks get pushed to next week, every week, because prior auths come first.

The agency: a two-line change becomes a ticket, a queue, and a review cycle.

The freelancer on call: paid per fix, so the site gets attention when something breaks and nothing in between.

Nobody: the site goes dark a couple of years after launch.

Pick by the work, not the title. A rebuild is a project. Keeping a site current and growing is a relationship, and most practices never staff it.

Phoenix Method is the fourth option: one practitioner you can text, running the website, SEO, GBP, and social as one job.
