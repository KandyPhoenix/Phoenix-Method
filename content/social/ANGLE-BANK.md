## "Repairs keep a site alive. They do not make it rank."

- Service tie-in: content SEO (monthly new pages, not just fixes). Blog: /blog/therapist-website-management.html

- Draft text (refresh the GSC number before using):

> Therapist website management is more than fixing what breaks.
>
> Ask whoever runs your practice site one question: what happens every month if I ask for nothing?
>
> If the answer is "nothing," your site is getting repairs. Repairs keep a site alive. They do not make it rank.
>
> Google ranks pages that answer what people actually search. A practice with an insurance page, a telehealth page and
> one page per specialty gives Google something to match. A homepage and a contact form does not.
>
> The searches exist. In the 90 days ending Oct 4, 2026, our own Google Search Console showed our site 208 times for
> "seo for mental health professionals." People are searching for this. The question is whose page they land on.
>
> That's why content SEO is part of every month we run a practice website. A small mental health practice needs new
> pages aimed at real patient searches, because Google can only rank a practice for the questions its site answers.
