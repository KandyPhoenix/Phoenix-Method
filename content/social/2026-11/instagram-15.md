## Instagram caption (paste-ready)

Your referrals Google you before they send anyone.

Primary care offices. School counselors. Case managers. Before they hand a family your number, they open your website.

They have one question: who here treats what? Teens or adults. Trauma, ADHD, eating disorders. Video or in person. Taking new clients or full.

A wall of headshots with two-sentence bios doesn't answer it. So the referral goes to the practice whose site does.

Put every clinician's specialties, ages and openings on your site, and keep them current. Then the people who refer to you can say yes in one visit, and the family calls you, not the practice down the road.
