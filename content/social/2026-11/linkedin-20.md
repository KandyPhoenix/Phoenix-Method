## Post (paste-ready)

Social media for private practice breaks at the link, not the post.

A post announces a new clinician. The link goes to the homepage. Now the reader has to dig through menus to find the person the post was about.

Two rules fix it:

1. Every post links to one page. The service page, the clinician page, the insurance page. Not the homepage.
2. Update that page before the post goes out. The new clinician gets a bio page first, then the announcement.

We hold ourselves to the same rule. Since October 9, all 7 posts we have published on LinkedIn and Facebook link to one specific blog post. None link to our homepage.

That's how done-for-you social posting works at Phoenix Method: every post is written from a page we have already built or updated, so the post and the page say the same thing. Social does not rank a small mental health practice by itself. It sends the right person to the right page, and that page does the ranking work.
