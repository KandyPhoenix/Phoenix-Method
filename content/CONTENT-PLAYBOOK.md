## 1. Who we are writing for (Kandy, 10-06-2026)

**The ideal client is a small mental health office** (solo therapist, 2–10 clinician group practice,
psychiatrist or psychiatric NP practice, counseling center, telehealth practice) **that has no one
running its website.** They need website management, content updates, SEO, Google Business Profile,
and social media handled by one practitioner they can text. We already do this for mental health
clients today, so every piece of content speaks to that owner.

**Their pain, in their words:** "our website is out of date", "we are not showing up on Google",
"we do not have time to post", "the agency we hired disappeared", "our new clinician is not on the site",
"insurance list is wrong on the website", "nobody can find our telehealth page".

**What we sell them:** the outsourced web + SEO + social department for a mental health practice:
website management and updates, technical SEO, local SEO and GBP, content SEO (blog + service pages),
AI Overview optimization, and done-for-you LinkedIn/Instagram posting. Every post ties back to one of these.

Every piece of content exists to do one of two things:
1. **Rank** — push a target keyword from page 3+ toward page 1 (blog posts, internal links).
2. **Convert** — get a practice owner to request the free audit (CTA on everything).

Nothing gets written "for engagement." If a post does not name a keyword and a service, rewrite it.

## 3. Voice — "Dead Rankings Rising"

Phoenix Method sounds like a practitioner who has done autopsies on a hundred dead websites and is
tired of agency fluff. Grungy, direct, a little dark, always backed by data. Talks to a practice owner
like a colleague, not a vendor.

**Site vocabulary (use it):** dead rankings, resurrect, autopsy and recon, battle plan, strike list,
"nothing hides, nothing survives unexamined", rise, compounding, "the kind of patient work that outranks
agencies charging three times as much", Search. Rise. Dominate.

**Sentence style:** short. Declarative. One idea per line. Fragments are fine. Second person ("your
practice page", "your clinicians"). Name the number. Name the page. No throat-clearing intros.

**Do:**
- Open with the problem the owner is living with ("Your practice page is on page 7. Nobody scrolls to page 7.")
- Use real numbers from GSC or Ahrefs and say where they came from
- Explain the *why* behind a Google behavior in plain words
- Reference the realities of a mental health practice: credentialing, insurance panels, telehealth,
  new clinicians, Psychology Today dependence, HIPAA-safe forms
- End every piece with the free audit CTA: https://www.phoenixmethodseo.com/#contact

**Don't (hard stops from CLAUDE.md):**
- No guarantees or outcome promises ("first page in 30 days", "double your traffic")
- No "crush / dominate overnight / guaranteed" hype in body copy (the brand tagline is the only exception)
- No Phoenix, Arizona. Ever. Location is The Woodlands, Texas; market is national.
- No invented stats, case studies, testimonials, or client names. Current clients are never named
  without Kandy's written OK.
- No patient details or hypothetical patient stories (HIPAA)
- No naming competitors negatively
- No keyword stuffing; no robotic AI cadence ("In today's digital landscape", "Let's dive in")
- No em-dash-every-sentence rhythm; vary it

## 4. Monthly blog posts — build spec

**Target:** the 1st-of-month post takes the next unused SEO spoke; the 16th post takes the next unused (A2)
website-management spoke, from the plan in §2 unless `gsc_keywords` shows a better opportunity
(impressions >= 100 and no page on the site already targeting it). Length 1,100–1,600 words. Exactly one
H1 containing the keyword. 4–6 H2s. One FAQ block (3 questions) with FAQPage schema. One callout.
One CTA block. Author box.

**Files to produce:**
1. `blog/<slug>.html` — copy `blog/tips-for-improving-website-rankings.html` as the template.
   Keep its `<head>`, `<style>`, nav/footer placeholders, and `site-chrome.js` include. Replace:
   - `<title>` 50–60 chars, keyword first, ends with ` | Phoenix Method` (no auto-append on this site)
   - meta description 140–160 chars, keyword + value + soft CTA
   - canonical: `https://www.phoenixmethodseo.com/blog/<slug>.html` (**www**, always)
   - og:*, twitter:*, article:published_time (today, ISO)
   - Article schema, BreadcrumbList schema, FAQPage schema
   - hero image path `/blog/images/<slug>.jpg` with descriptive alt text (Kandy supplies the image;
     put the image brief in the PR body and email, see §7)
   - body sections, `.blog-meta` category + date (format "October 1, 2026")
2. `blog/index.html` — add a card for the new post at the top of the featured cards grid, matching the
   existing card markup exactly (category label, H3 title, excerpt, date • READ NOW). Do not touch the
   `PHOENIX-AI-MANIFEST` / `phoenix-ai-articles` block; that is machine-managed.
3. `feed.xml` — add an `<item>` at the top (title, link, guid, pubDate RFC-822, description).
4. `sitemap.xml` — add the new URL (www) with `<lastmod>` today.
5. (1st-of-month post only) `blog/tips-for-improving-website-rankings.html` — append an "Updated <Month Year>" paragraph
   before "The Bottom Line" that links to the new post, and bump `article:modified_time` + `dateModified`.
6. `workers/portal/public/pm/data.json` — append a worklog entry `{"date","type":"content","title","ticket"}`
   and mark the matching `blog_topics` entry `"status":"drafted"` if one exists.

**Internal links (required):** the mental health pillar `/industries/mental-health-seo/`, one service page
(`/services/technical-seo/`, `/services/content-seo/`, `/services/local-seo/`, `/services/gbp-optimization/`,
`/services/ai-overview-optimization/`, `/services/seo-development/`), and one other blog post.
Check every href exists in the repo before committing.

**Delivery:** branch `PM-<ticket>-blog-<slug>`, PR to `main` with reviewer `KandyPhoenix`, **do not merge**.
Email Kandy (see §7). Kandy reviews, then tells Claude to merge.

## 5. LinkedIn posts (10th and 20th) — spec

Audience: therapy and psychiatry practice owners, group practice directors, clinic operators.
Tone: authoritative practitioner. Length 900–1,300 characters. Structure:

1. **Hook line** (<= 12 words, stops the scroll, contains or implies the keyword)
2. blank line
3. **3–5 short lines of real value** — one concrete mechanism, one number with its source
4. **The service tie-in:** name ONE Phoenix Method service and say in one sentence why a small
   mental health practice needs it to rank for the month's keyword
   (10th = technical / website management / local-GBP side; 20th = content / authority / social side)
5. **The blog link** — this month's blog post URL, framed as "the full breakdown"
6. 3–5 hashtags: #MentalHealthSEO #TherapistSEO #PrivatePractice #SEO plus one topical

The 10th post introduces the month's blog post. The 20th post takes one section of it deeper or
answers an objection ("we already have Psychology Today", "we can't afford an agency").
LinkedIn articles and public posts are indexed by Google, so the keyword should appear naturally
in the first line.

**Image:** suggest one. Write a ChatGPT image prompt (dark background, ember/orange #FF4D00 accent,
grungy texture, one bold stat or phrase, no faces, 1200x627). Kandy generates it.

Save to `content/social/YYYY-MM/linkedin-DD.md`. Email Kandy the paste-ready text + image prompt.
