Frequently Asked

Pricing Questions

How much should a small business spend on SEO?

Most small businesses are well-served between $500 and $1,500 per month for ongoing SEO, plus a one-time setup of $800-$1,200. Below $500/mo, you're getting template-and-forget service. Above $2,000/mo is usually agency overhead a small business doesn't need.

Why is small business SEO pricing so variable across providers?

Because large agencies build in overhead, sales commissions, and account management layers that don't produce rankings. A smaller, focused team without that structure can charge less and deliver more. The quality of SEO work doesn't scale linearly with price — the right $900/mo engagement often outperforms a $3,000/mo agency relationship for a small business.

Is there a minimum contract length?

No. All Phoenix Method retainers are month-to-month. You can cancel any time. We'd rather keep earning your business every month than lock you into something you regret.

What's included vs what costs extra?

Monthly retainer includes everything in that plan's deliverables (technical audit, rank tracking, GBP monitoring, reports, content hours, etc.). Anything beyond the plan (major redesigns, large content pushes, paid ad work) is quoted separately. Retainer clients get 25% off à la carte services and hourly rates for add-on work.

Can I cancel and come back later?

Yes. All plans are month-to-month — no contracts, no penalties. If you need to stop, stop. If you want to come back, we'll pick up where you left off. Just know SEO compounds: a gap means competitors gain ground, and you'll spend some time rebuilding momentum. Staying in Spark during slow periods is usually cheaper than fully stopping and restarting.

Do you offer discounts for annual payment?

No. We'd rather price fairly month-to-month than ask for annual commitment. Annual discounts from SEO providers often correlate with weak retention — they need the upfront commitment because clients leave after they see the work.

What payment methods do you accept?

All payments go through Square. Credit card, debit card, and ACH supported. Invoices auto-generate each month. No crypto, no wire transfers, no weird stuff.
