Common Questions

Frequently Asked

What is SEO and why does my business need it?

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) is the practice of improving your website's visibility in Google search results. When potential customers search for services you offer, SEO ensures your business appears at the top instead of your competitors. For healthcare and therapy practices, this is critical — 77% of patients use search engines before booking an appointment. Without SEO, you're invisible to the people actively looking for what you offer.

How long does it take to see SEO results?

Most businesses see measurable improvements in 3-6 months, with significant results between 6-12 months. Technical fixes and local SEO optimizations can produce faster wins within 30-60 days. SEO is a compounding investment — the longer you maintain a consistent strategy, the stronger and more durable your rankings become. Unlike paid ads, organic rankings keep working for you even when you're not actively spending.

How does Google's AI Overview affect SEO in 2026?

Google's AI Overviews generate summary answers at the top of search results. To appear in these, your content needs strong E-E-A-T signals, clear structure with proper schema markup, and comprehensive answers to specific questions. Phoenix Method optimizes content for AI Overview visibility alongside traditional organic rankings.
