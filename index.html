Common Questions

Frequently Asked

What is SEO and why does my business need it?

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) is the practice of improving your website's visibility in Google search results. When potential customers search for services you offer, SEO ensures your business appears at the top instead of your competitors. For healthcare and therapy practices, this is critical — 77% of patients use search engines before booking an appointment. Without SEO, you're invisible to the people actively looking for what you offer.

How long does it take to see SEO results?

There's no honest answer that gives you a specific date — anyone promising "page one in 30 days" is either lying or selling something that'll backfire. SEO is a compounding investment: foundation work builds on itself, rankings stabilize over time, and the real return comes from consistency. Some technical fixes produce quick visibility gains. Others take months to reflect in rankings. We don't promise timelines we can't control — we promise transparent reporting and a strategy built to last.

What makes Phoenix Method different from other SEO agencies?

Most agencies hand you off to a junior account manager and run templated reports. Phoenix Method doesn't work that way — every campaign is built from scratch, every strategy is custom, and you're working directly with the people doing the actual work.

We also don't make promises we can't back with data. No guaranteed rankings, no inflated projections — just transparent monthly reporting, real keyword movement, and strategies that compound over time. We monitor Google algorithm updates in real-time and adapt before your competitors even read the news.

How does Google's AI Overview affect SEO in 2026?

Google's AI Overviews generate summary answers at the top of search results. To appear in these, your content needs strong E-E-A-T signals, clear structure with proper schema markup, and comprehensive answers to specific questions. Phoenix Method optimizes content for AI Overview visibility alongside traditional organic rankings.
